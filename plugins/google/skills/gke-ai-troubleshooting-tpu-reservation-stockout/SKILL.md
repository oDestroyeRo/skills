name: gke-ai-troubleshooting-tpu-reservation-stockout
description: >-
  Diagnose and resolve cluster-wide GKE TPU node pool creation or scale-up
  failures caused by Compute Engine reservation stockouts (GCE_STOCKOUT,
  ZONE_RESOURCE_POOL_EXHAUSTED) when existing unhealthy or NotReady TPU node
  pools in the cluster have node auto-repair disabled and hold reserved TPU
  capacity. Use when TPU node pool creation or scale-up fails with GCE_STOCKOUT,
  ZONE_RESOURCE_POOL_EXHAUSTED, or ZONE_RESOURCE_POOL_EXHAUSTED_WITH_DETAILS
  despite having a specific Compute Engine TPU reservation. Don't use for
  general pod scheduling without a TPU reservation or workload-level container
  crashes.
